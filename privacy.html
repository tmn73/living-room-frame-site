Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026.

Living Room Frame is a private app for one household. It runs on a photo frame in that household's home.

What the app reads from Google

  • Google Photos: only the photos that a person picks in the Google Photos picker. The app cannot see any other photo.
  • Google Calendar: read-only access to the events of the household's calendars, to show them on the frame. The app does not create, change or delete events.

Where the data goes

Picked photos and calendar events are stored only on the frame, inside the home. The app does not send them to any server, does not share them with anyone and does not use them for advertising or analytics. The app has no analytics.

How to stop it

Remove the app's access at any time from your Google account, under Security, "Third-party apps with account access". Photos already on the frame can be removed from the frame itself.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
